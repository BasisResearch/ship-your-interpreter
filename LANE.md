  gives it) and will need `errnoOwn` in their specs; `wp_oomBlock` and
  `wp_mainErrTail` already carry `errnoOwn` in their preconditions, so it can be
  threaded through `fwrite`/`fprintf` directly.

  (`gen_interp_steps.py --table stdio`) on merge instead of merging generated parts.
- **Import hazard.** `VsaIris/Vsa/Stdout/Tac.lean` adds global `sx_side`/`nx_addr`
  macro rules and `SymJalr` a `jalrStep_of_obs` that shadows the interpreter's. Any
  interpreter-proof file importing them breaks (measured: `CallJalr`, `LoopArgs`,
  `UnaryNegT`, `Binary*T`, `ProofValue*`). Import the stdio layer only at the top
  (`Interp/Supply.lean`), as `ExitH/Iris.lean` is. It also brings
  `VsaIris.Interp.calleeSaved` (which includes `sp`) into scope, where files in
  `namespace VsaIris.Interp` that open `Newlib` mean `Newlib.calleeSaved`.
