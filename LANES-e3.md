Each case takes the children's specs (`evalSpecT_body`, or the Löb hypothesis
`evalSpecsP`) and the helper specs (`valueTruthySpec`, `valueBoolSpec`,
`valueIntSpec`, for `twpW`/`wpW`) as hypotheses. `caseP_UnaryNeg` follows E2's
error-arm design: it also takes `valueKindNameSpec` and `ErrEnv`, and its
statement is `evalSpecsP … ∗ errCtx inp ⊢ evalSpecP_body …` (package A supplies
`errCtx` after `interp_run`'s `setjmp`). The type-error row runs the operand,
the failed kind test, `value_kind_name` (`ms_callKindName`) and
`runtime_error(in, line, "operand of '%s' must be an int, got %s", "-", name)`
(`ms_rtErrEval`, with `operand_fmt`), which aborts to the arm's
`abortAt Core s (evalNeed …)` and hands back the result slot.

The partial logical and `neg` cases split on the actual left value (its
truthiness after `value_truthy`, or its kind). The long row (`LogicalAndTrue`, `LogicalOrFalse`) continues
the matching truthiness and exports the other one. The short row continues
that export (`#ix_piece … from <Long>P_p2 at 2`), and `caseP_LogicalAnd`/
`caseP_LogicalOr` close each operator by discharging the long row's export.

## Shared layer: `VsaIris/Interp/ArmLogical.lean`
- `logNode_of_repr` (a `BinNode` at tag 7), `UnNode`/`unNode_of_repr`/`unView`
  (tag 8, one child at `+16`).
- `EvalSaved3`: the prologue's spills (`ra`, `s0`-`s2`) that the non-binary
  arms keep. G's `EvalSaved` also requires `s3`, which only the binary arm
  spills. Includes `.store`, `.agree` and `ix_saved3`. **Other E lanes: the
  leaf, var, call and fn arms will need this too.**
- `ms_callTruthy`: calls `value_truthy` on a value the run copied into its
  own frame bytes. It carves the slot as `valAt` from the three words'
  `valOf`, calls the helper and joins the slot back. The continuation gets the
  bit in `a0` and bytes that agree outside the slot.
- `iw_regFact`: a run from a state whose register `k` is known to hold `v`.
  `ix_run`'s `using` facts rewrite loaded values, not branch conditions over
  an input register, so a branch on `a0` after a call is otherwise not pruned.
- `ix_fwdF hoff`: store forwarding at frame addresses in `s.toNat - 1088 + c`
  form. It leaves out `ldv_store_hit`, whose repeated-address match against
  two such sums is a defeq check that unfolds `Nat.sub` and times out.
- `boolBit_ne`, `seqzV`/`seqzBit_ne`, `toInt_neg_wrap`.

## Findings
- `evalPre`/`world` do not carry what `runtime_error` needs (`binImg`, an
  aligned `jmp_buf` `ra` word, `InpGeom`). H2 and E2 found the same gap; E2's
  `errCtx`/`ErrEnv` fills it and E3 uses it.
- `#ix_piece … from P at k` fixes the continuing piece's binders to `P`'s, so
  the premises an error row needs must already be on the row it continues
  (`caseP_UnaryNegInt` carries `valueKindNameSpec`, `ErrEnv`, `errCtx`).
